Multi-property organic growth program

A portfolio of WordPress service sites with crawl waste, thin templates, and inconsistent service/location coverage needed a repeatable SEO operating system — not one-off page edits.

Challenge

  • Index bloat and competing near-duplicate URLs
  • Weak service/location page templates
  • No shared keyword or internal-linking standard across properties

Approach

  • Full technical audit: crawl, Search Console, sitemap, canonicals
  • Standardized on-page modules for titles, H1s, FAQs, and CTAs
  • Topic clusters + internal links mapped to commercial intent
  • Monthly indexation and ranking QA across the portfolio

Outcome

  • Cleaner indexation and reduced soft-404 / orphan risk
  • Priority service pages ranking with stronger SERP coverage
  • Editorial team shipping SEO-ready pages from templates

A lead-gen WordPress site was losing ground on mobile experience: heavy theme debt, uncontrolled plugins, and LCP issues on money pages — while marketing still needed flexible publishing.

Outcome

  • Lower operational risk for ongoing SEO work

Local service SEO & conversion architecture

A regional service business competed in crowded local SERPs with thin city pages and weak differentiation. The goal: pages that rank and convert — backed by clean technical signals.

Challenge

  • Doorway-style location pages with little unique value
  • Inconsistent NAP / entity signals across the site
  • Titles and intros not aligned to commercial search intent

Approach

  • Service × location information architecture
  • Unique proof blocks: process, FAQs, service specifics
  • LocalBusiness / Service schema and internal link scaffolding
  • CTR-focused title/meta testing with Search Console review

Outcome

  • Stronger visibility on high-intent local queries
  • Landing pages built for calls and form conversions
  • A reusable playbook for new service markets
